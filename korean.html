명조체 타이틀 (Myungjo Title)

This is a subtitle using Nanum Myeongjo Bold.

The Myungjo (Myeongjo) font style is characterized by its high contrast between horizontal and vertical strokes and its elegant serifs, similar to "Mincho" in Japanese or "Songti" in Chinese.
